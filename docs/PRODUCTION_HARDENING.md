**Status:** consolidated, de-duplicated backlog from a six-dimension read-only audit (Security, Reliability & Resilience, Performance & Scale, Data Integrity & Multi-Tenancy, Observability & Ops, Frontend). Every finding was re-verified against source at HEAD in the main working tree — file:line and quoted code confirmed. Repo left untouched.

**Audit date:** 2026-07-29 · **Scope:** `backend-dotnet/Zayra.Api` (.NET 8), `frontend/` (Next.js), `render.yaml`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.

Paths are relative to `backend-dotnet/Zayra.Api/` unless prefixed with `frontend/`, `render.yaml`, or `.github/`.

## 1. Production-readiness verdict + top risks

**Verdict: NOT production-ready. 6 P0 blockers must be closed first.**

This is a genuinely mature codebase — it has absorbed several prior audit waves and the *foundations are strong*: interface-driven tenant query filters with a boot assertion, company-scope StrictMode forced fail-closed in production, JWT/SeedAdmin production fail-fast, SHA-256 hashed refresh/SCIM tokens, per-tenant lockout + per-IP rate limiting, centralized CSV formula-injection escaping, disciplined `IgnoreQueryFilters()` re-validation, default-deny authorization, append-only audit hash chain, error boundaries + single-flight token refresh on the frontend, and `AddDbContextPool` + `EnableRetryOnFailure`. None of those are findings; do not re-audit them.

The blocking gaps concentrate in **five themes**, each of which has at least one P0:

1. **Payroll correctness at scale (P0-2, P0-3).** The single most important financial operation — `PayrollController.Process` — is both *non-atomic* (silent loan/advance over-collection on any mid-run cancel/crash, and un-reprocessable afterward) and *N+1-bound* (statutory rules re-queried per employee → request timeout on large tenants). Same method, two independent P0s.
2. **Authorization completeness + data classification in recruitment (P0-1).** Read endpoints are gated only by class-level `[Authorize]` while writes require HR roles, and `Candidate`/`JobApplication`/`OfferLetter` are `ITenantOwned` only — so any employee reads every company's candidate PII and offer salaries group-wide.
3. **Deploy & data durability (P0-4, P0-5).** `autoDeploy: true` ships migration-bearing commits with **no migrate step** → `42703` 500s tenant-wide; and uploaded compliance documents are written to the container's **ephemeral disk** on `plan: free` → permanent loss on every restart.
4. **Privacy / PDPL-GDPR (P0-6).** Employee names and company legal names are POSTed to a **third-party public crowd-sourced translation API** (MyMemory) on keystroke, with no consent, DPA, or opt-out.
5. **Enterprise operability (P1 cluster).** No error telemetry/APM, no structured logging or correlation IDs, no distributed lease on background workers (Qiwa double-push), inline SMTP with no timeout, and an over-broad exception→400 mapping that both leaks internals and hides real 500s from alerting.

**Top 5 risks, plain-language:** (1) employees silently over-charged on loans and payroll runs that time out on big tenants; (2) any logged-in employee can read the whole group's hiring pipeline and salaries; (3) a routine schema-changing merge takes every tenant down with no rollback runbook; (4) uploaded HR/compliance documents vanish on the next restart; (5) staff PII leaks to a public translation corpus.
