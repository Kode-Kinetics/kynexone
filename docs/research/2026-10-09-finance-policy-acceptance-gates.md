# Finance acceptance gates for configurable HR policies

Research date: **October 9, 2026**. This follows the [HR policy and GCC leave memo](2026-10-09-flexible-hr-policies-and-gcc-leave.md). The gates below are recommendations, not completed tests or a claim that the current application supports every configuration. Country applicability, effective policy version, employment terms, and the entity's reporting framework must be explicit.

| Finance concern | Verified source or configuration pattern | Concrete acceptance gate — recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Pay frequency and wage protection** | Saudi Labor Law Art. 90 distinguishes wage payment intervals by worker category. HRSD reduced the permitted WPS file submission period to 30 days effective March 1, 2025. These are separate obligations. [HRSD wage provisions](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%81%D9%87), [WPS announcement](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/fil/node/5573339). | Separate accrual period, cutoff, processing date, contractual payment due date, bank settlement, and WPS submission/acceptance. Never label the upload window as permission to pay late. A custom calendar must cover each employee/date once, avoid overlaps/gaps, handle bank holidays, and preserve closed runs. |
| **Payroll reconciliation** | HRSD's upload service processes pay/reconciliation information and exposes compliance irregularities for review. [WPS upload service](https://prod.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%B9-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%81-%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%88%D8%B1). | Reconcile earnings components minus deductions to net pay, then employee totals to payroll/bank/WPS totals. Distinguish generated, approved, submitted, rejected, and paid states. Prevent duplicate payments on retry and link adjustments to the original run. A downloaded file is not proof of payment or acceptance. |
| **Leave accrual, availability, and liability** | Oracle models accrual, vesting, proration, and final disbursement independently. IAS 19 addresses employee-benefit cost and liabilities arising from employee service; the applicable accounting treatment depends on benefit type. [Oracle rules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/oapff/formulas-for-accrual-plans.html), [IFRS Foundation IAS 19](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-19-employee-benefits/). | Report earned, available-to-use, taken, carried, adjusted, and payable-on-exit balances separately. Do not calculate accounting liability merely from the ESS available balance. Finance must approve its valuation basis. Test hire/exit fractions, leap February, thresholds, negative/advanced leave, and service-band changes. Existing posted accruals need explicit adjustments, not silent recalculation. |
| **Overtime wage basis** | Saudi Art. 107 describes hourly wage plus 50% of basic wage; compensatory leave requires employee consent under implementing rules. [HRSD overtime provisions](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%81%D9%87). | Test separately stored basic and other wage components, the approved hourly divisor, daily/weekly thresholds, holidays, rounding, and caps. Compare payroll output with a finance-approved example where basic and total wage differ. A blanket 1.5× total-pay formula must not masquerade as the Saudi rule. Compensatory hours/days must not also pay as cash unintentionally. |
| **Grade budgets and benefit contributions** | Oracle grade rates identify currency/frequency and minimum/midpoint/maximum. Eligibility profiles can use grade, entity, payroll, employment, and service criteria. [Grade rates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/fagrl/overview-of-grade-rates.html), [Eligibility](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/faiwc/eligibility-profiles.html). | Separate salary-band guidance from actual salary and total employer cost. Validate frequency/currency conversion, employee deductions, employer contributions, benefit caps and effective dates. Reuse existing approved benefit records without duplicating enrollment or payroll charges. Show headcount-weighted cost changes by entity/cost center; treat AI amounts as proposals. |
| **Policy change auditability** | Oracle documents effective dates and calendars in payroll definitions; its HCM rules use explicit routing conditions. [Payroll calendars](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/fahbo/payroll-definitions-and-time-periods-overview.html), [Approval routing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/faucf/list-builders.html). | Record actor, approver, reason, scope, effective date, before/after values, source and version. Preview affected employees and monetary changes. Resolve overlapping group policies deterministically. Apply prospective changes without rewriting historical postings; route retroactive changes through a traceable correction. Test unauthorized cross-entity edits and missing approvers. |

Minimum finance sign-off evidence: a published policy version; representative calculation examples approved by the responsible owner; a scoped payroll rehearsal; reconciled leave and payroll ledgers; bank/WPS validation evidence where applicable; and a demonstrated correction path. The HR interface should expose the business decision and its consequence while retaining the underlying evidence for finance and audit.
