# Flexible HR setup and GCC annual leave

Research date: **October 9, 2026**. Official government and enterprise-product documentation only; no signed-in competitor testing or application changes. This is configuration research for ordinary private-sector employment, not certification of every worker category, special jurisdiction, contract, or employer policy. A country preset needs an approved legal scope and effective date before activation. Kuwait's current consolidated wording remains unverified below.

**The essential distinction:** a leave year defines the account/reporting boundary; it does not define how leave is earned, when it can be used, or how a partial year is settled. Oracle exposes separate anniversary, accrual, vesting, proration, carryover, and final-disbursement rules. SAP separately supports regular accrual and hire-period rules. These are documented configuration patterns, not legal rules. [Oracle accrual-plan rules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/oapff/formulas-for-accrual-plans.html), [SAP hire rules](https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-employee-central/implementing-time-management-in-sap-successfactors/hire-rule).

| Country | Verified annual-leave baseline | Configuration consequence — recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Saudi Arabia** | Labor Law Art. 109: at least 21 days annually, increasing to 30 after five continuous years with the employer. Art. 111 provides pay for unused accrued leave on departure and proportional leave pay for fractions of the year worked. [HRSD Arts. 109–111](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%81%D9%87). | Preserve service bands and partial-year settlement. The reviewed provisions do not specify one monthly posting formula or a January account reset. Do not label 21/12 as a statutory monthly rule. |
| **UAE** | Federal private-sector guidance: 30 days after one year; two days per month for service exceeding six months but below one year; final-year fractions are recognized. Part-time entitlement follows actual working hours and implementing rules. [Official annual-leave guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/types-of-leaves-and-entitlements-in-the-private-sector/annual-leave). | Model first-year service thresholds and part-time rules separately. A universal 2.5-days-per-month preset would conceal these distinctions. |
| **Qatar** | Art. 79: after a continuous year, at least three weeks below five years' service and four weeks at five years or more; fractions of a year are proportional. Art. 81 provides payment for due unused leave on termination. [Art. 79](https://www.almeezan.qa/LawArticles.aspx?LawArticleID=51873&LawId=3961&language=en), [Art. 81](https://www.almeezan.qa/LawArticles.aspx?LawArticleID=51875&LawID=3961&language=en). | Preserve the statutory unit, weeks, and explicitly define any day conversion. The cited article does not label days as working or calendar days. |
| **Oman** | RD 53/2023, Art. 78: at least 30 days with gross pay, with use restricted until six months after joining. Art. 81 provides gross pay for unused balance at service end. These provisions do not state a monthly accrual formula. [Ministry of Labour, Arts. 78–81](https://mol.gov.om/Laborlaw?lid=6). | Keep the earliest use date separate from earning/posting rules. Do not invent a statutory 2.5-day monthly rate from the annual figure. |
| **Bahrain** | Law 36/2012, Art. 58 expressly states at least 30 paid days, 2.5 per month, and proportional leave below one year's service. Art. 59 governs remaining-balance payment and includes a written-refusal exception. [Official law, Arts. 58–59](https://lloc.gov.bh/Legislation/HTM/K3612). | Do not implement the one-year phrase as zero first-year accrual. Preserve settlement conditions rather than treating all unused balances identically. |
| **Kuwait** | Available official sources conflict in currency: an older English text has a nine-month first-year threshold, while an official parliamentary report reproduces amended wording. The current consolidated PDFs could not be retrieved. [PAM consolidation](https://www.manpower.gov.kw/docs/LaborLaw/E_LaborLaw.pdf), [government legislative report](https://www.e.gov.kw/sites/KGOArabic/Pages/ApplicationPages/NewsDetail.aspx?nid=979655). | Leave the preset review-required. A parliamentary report alone does not prove final enacted/current wording; do not ship the older threshold as current. |

**Recommended leave policy model.** Store scope and effective dates; annual entitlement/service bands; account year (calendar, anniversary, or custom start); accrual frequency and posting timing; hire/termination/transfer proration; day/hour unit and holiday/rest-day counting; earliest use/advance limits; rounding; carryover/expiry; and settlement pay basis. Financial year and leave year may differ. Policy assignment should explain which legal entity, location, grade, employment group, or explicit employee population it covers, and resolve overlapping assignments visibly.

An illustrative calculation shows why the controls cannot be combined: with a **chosen** 30-day annual policy, calendar year 2026, and July 1 start, actual-calendar-day proration is `30 × 184/365 = 15.1233…`; six complete monthly portions give `30 × 6/12 = 15`. These are arithmetic examples, not legal defaults. Display the selected denominator, inclusive dates, precision, and rounding. An anniversary account has a different boundary; it does not remove partial-service obligations.

**Recommended enterprise configuration patterns:**

| Area | Evidence and proposed behavior |
|---|---|
| **Attendance** | Oracle configures device/Web Clock behavior through profiles. Allow several permitted capture methods per population, with an explicit default/fallback and connected custom integration. Define correction approval, timezone, duplicate handling, and evidence requirements; a custom label alone is not an integration. [Time-entry profile options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/faitl/time-collection-device-and-web-clock-integration-options-on-time-entry-profiles.html). |
| **Overtime** | Oracle separates worker-group rules and overtime periods from time-card periods. Offer paid, compensatory, mixed, and custom policies with thresholds, day categories, rate basis, caps, and approval. [Processing profiles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/faitp/how-many-time-processing-objects-to-create.html). Saudi Art. 107 describes hourly wage plus 50% of basic wage and compensatory leave with employee consent under regulations; generic 1.5× total pay is not equivalent. [HRSD Art. 107](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%81%D9%87). |
| **Salary grades** | Oracle grade rates carry minimum, midpoint, maximum, currency, and frequency. Provide manual creation/import first, with optional AI proposals entering the same editable draft and approval path. Do not represent generated bands as market benchmarks without sourced compensation data. [Grade rates](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/fagrl/overview-of-grade-rates.html). |
| **Approvals** | Oracle supports rule conditions and group, job-level, position, and supervisory routing. Configure policies per transaction/population, with ordered stages, fallback owners, and a sample-route preview; one company-wide dropdown is insufficient. [HCM routing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/faucf/list-builders.html). |
| **Pay cycles** | Oracle separates frequency, payroll calendar, cutoff, processing, and payment dates. Custom cycles need generated, validated periods assigned to employee populations. [Payroll calendars](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/fahbo/payroll-definitions-and-time-periods-overview.html). Saudi Art. 90 distinguishes payment timing by wage category, so arbitrary schedules require country validation. [HRSD Art. 90](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%81%D9%87). |
| **Benefits** | Oracle uses reusable eligibility profiles, including employment, grade, payroll, entity, and service criteria. Link existing approved HR policies; let users import or create missing ones and review eligibility, caps, contributions, and payroll treatment. AI may propose mappings; it must not silently replace existing entitlements. [Eligibility profiles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/faiwc/eligibility-profiles.html). |

**Delivery priorities.** First inventory and reuse existing policy records, then add typed editors and effective-dated group assignment. Connect setup choices to the actual leave, attendance, approval, benefits, and payroll engines before calling them configured. Support explicit inheritance and conflict review. A “Custom” choice must open an executable, validated configuration rather than store unexplained text.

Validate mid-year hires/exits, leap years, service-band changes, unpaid periods, group transfers, overlapping policies, overnight/multiple-source attendance, missing approvers, and irregular payroll dates. Show before/after balances and payroll effects, retain approved historical versions, and require domain-owner review before application. These are recommended acceptance scenarios, not completed tests.
