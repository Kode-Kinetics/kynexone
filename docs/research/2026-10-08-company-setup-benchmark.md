# Company setup benchmark

Research date: **October 8, 2026**. Scope: public official documentation for six HR platforms, reviewed by the HR implementation and enterprise configuration research workstreams. No signed-in competitor testing was performed. These sources establish documented capabilities and service methods, not comparative usability, performance, accessibility, or customer outcomes. Availability can depend on subscription, region, release, implementation route, and permissions.

The recommendation is to make company setup a guided implementation process with understandable decisions, clear ownership, recoverable imports, and evidence of readiness. “Draft generated,” “configuration applied,” and “ready to operate” are separate outcomes.

**Current local redesign.** The implementation branch separates Guided setup, Import organization, and Manage settings, with settings grouped by purpose. Guided setup has four stages: Company details, Working week, People & pay, and Review & create. Imports prioritize a package upload; individual CSV files, formats, and audit metadata use disclosures. Validation findings and application results remain available. These are local source changes, not a production-release or readiness claim. Sources: [setup navigation](../../frontend/src/views/SetupPage.tsx), [guided setup](../../frontend/src/components/AiSetupAssistant.tsx), [organization import](../../frontend/src/components/OrgStructureImportPanel.tsx).

**Next-phase requirements — recommendations, not implemented capabilities:**

1. **Next: persisted ownership and resume.** Store drafts, task owners, dependencies, and completion evidence server-side. Distinguish required, optional, blocked, and awaiting another owner. Switching views without losing mounted state is not durable resume after sign-out or device changes.
2. **Next: true structure review.** Show proposed and existing legal entities, branches, reporting departments, cost centers, grades, and designations with their actual relationships. Include material changes and unresolved associations. Confirm the domain model before building a graphical editor.
3. **Next: readiness gates.** Verify a sample hire, approval routing, correctly scoped ESS access, and payroll rehearsal before a responsible owner signs off. Workday’s deployment methodology separately describes end-to-end/parallel testing and go-live checklist sign-off; it does not establish a universal in-product readiness dashboard. [Deployment guide](https://forms.workday.com/content/dam/web/en-us/documents/other/deployment-strategies-guide-enus1.pdf).
4. **Later: spreadsheet mapping and correction.** Accept familiar exports, suggest editable mappings, preview normalized values, and return actionable correction files. Oracle documents row messages and correction/reupload in its spreadsheet loader. [Spreadsheet recovery](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/fahdl/how-data-is-uploaded-using-hcm-spreadsheet-data-loader.html). This requires stable identities, schemas, localization, and deterministic validation.

**Recommended guardrails.** Navigation is not authorization: preserve server permissions and entity scope on every operation. Give HR ownership of structure/policies, payroll ownership of pay configuration, and IT ownership of access/integrations. Link IT-managed existing work email to the employee account. Keep AI suggestions distinguishable from confirmed policy. Country defaults and generated drafts must not silently establish employment or statutory policy. Preserve validation-before-apply, payload invalidation, audit records, and reconciliation; explain partial failures. Require payroll-owner review of currency, grade bands, pay components, effective dates, and statutory assumptions, followed by a controlled payroll rehearsal. These are KynexOne design principles, not claims that every competitor implements them identically.

**Acceptance metrics — proposed and unmeasured.** Establish a baseline before setting numerical targets. Measure first-task success without assistance; time and attempts to resolve a representative import error; accurate distinction between draft/applied/ready; and task completion by HR, payroll, and IT personas. Require loss-free resume across reload/sign-out for the future persistence feature, keyboard completion, English/Arabic readability, and actionable section/row/field error messages. Verify that unauthorized writes fail, changed payloads require revalidation, applied results reconcile, and readiness evidence links to executed operational scenarios. Do not substitute a completion percentage for those checks.
