## Delivery direction

Connect one approved source to reviewable configuration and employee explanations. Every accepted setting should retain source clause/version; coverage must distinguish extracted, missing, review-required and unsupported. New policy versions should ultimately show configuration differences, employee impact and financial scenarios before activation. Kody should distinguish policy wording, employee applicability and calculated live balances, escalating disagreements.

Do not claim competitors cannot configure from policies merely because public evidence is absent. No reviewed source establishes reliable arbitrary-handbook-to-every-module automation. Deliver bounded operational coverage and expose the remainder honestly.
